# Crash Kitchen
### Description
Crash Kitchen is a fast-paced, 2 person VR co-op game. The goal of the game is for one person to drive the food truck to the destionation, while the other player cooks in the back. The cook does not know the food order or the recipies, and it is up to the driver to relay that information, while quickly driving around obstacles. We did extensive playtesting, so that our game would not have VR sickness. We tested both on people who were prone to VR sickness from other games, and people who were not. We found that our game did not, in any way (~50 people tested), get motion/VR sickness. In general, players found the game fun and engaging, and loved the chaotic teamworking aspects.
